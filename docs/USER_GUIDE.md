# FamilyShield AI — user guide

This guide is written for parents and family members who are not technical. The goal is to help you pause, check, and talk before money or personal information is shared.

## First steps

1. Register with your email and a strong password.
2. Create a family group from **Settings**. Give it a name your family recognizes.
3. Copy the invite code from **Settings** and share it only with people you trust.
4. Ask family members to register and choose **Join a family** in Settings using that code.
5. The person who creates the family is an administrator. Administrators can change member roles.

## Checking a suspicious message

1. Go to **Message Scanner**.
2. Paste the message, or upload a `.txt`/`.eml` file.
3. If you want the whole family to see the alert, choose your family before scanning.
4. Press **Analyze message**.
5. Read the result:
   - **High Risk (red):** several strong warning signs. Do not act on the message.
   - **Suspicious (amber):** be careful and verify through a trusted channel.
   - **Safe (green):** no obvious warning signs were detected. This is **not** a guarantee.
6. Use **Share with family** if you want other members to review it.

What the result shows:
- **Risk score** — a 0–100 advisory score.
- **Indicators** — the exact phrases or patterns that raised concern.
- **Explanation** — why those patterns matter in plain language.
- **Recommended actions** — what to do next.

## Checking a link

1. Go to **URL Scanner** and paste the full link.
2. Do not open the link on your phone or computer first.
3. Press **Analyze URL**.
4. Review the structural findings, HTTPS/TLS information, and any available reputation data.

FamilyShield analyzes links on its server. It does not open the page in your browser and does not run anything from the page. If threat intelligence is not configured, the result clearly says it is unavailable.

## Family alerts

When a scan is suspicious or high risk and is filed under a family, everyone in that family can see an alert on the **Family Alerts** page. Open an alert to read the summary and the underlying scan. Press **Mark as reviewed** once the family has discussed it.

## Dashboard

The **Overview** page shows real counts and charts from your saved scans and family alerts: total scans, high-risk detections, weekly trend, common scam categories, and family activity. It starts empty until you or your family run scans.

## Scan history

Use **Scan History** to search past scans, filter by risk level or scan type, open details, or delete a scan. Deleting a scan does not delete alerts that were already shared.

## Awareness center

The **Scam Awareness** section explains common Indian UPI scams, OTP fraud, fake customer care numbers, digital arrest scams, fake investments, social engineering, and a safety checklist for parents. Share these articles with family members, especially older relatives.

## Golden rules

- Never share an OTP, PIN, password, or card number with anyone who contacted you.
- Do not pay because of urgency, threats, or a countdown timer.
- Verify payment requests by calling the person or company on a number you already trust.
- Bank and government officials do not ask for OTPs or demand immediate digital payment.
- If in doubt, stop and discuss it in your family group before acting.

## Limitations

FamilyShield uses rules, an optional local classifier, and optional external services. It can miss new scams and can sometimes flag legitimate messages. It is an aid, not a guarantee, and does not replace careful verification.
